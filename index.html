What the audit actually catches

Illustrative examples, not real client quotes

I won't put fake reviews on this page. Real client results will replace these the moment I have them — for now, here's the kind of thing a Full System Audit actually finds, using the same categories every real roadmap covers.

Offer · illustrative example

Found: single product, no bundle, nothing pushing a decision today.

Fix: a simple 2–3 unit bundle with a small per-unit discount, plus real stock-based urgency.

Typical impact: bundling alone often lifts average order value 15–25%, with zero added ad spend.

Website / CRO · illustrative example

Found: shipping cost only revealed at the final checkout step.

Fix: show shipping cost or a free-shipping threshold on the product page itself, plus trust signals near the buy button.

Typical impact: checkout completion is often the single biggest leak — fixing it converts existing traffic, no extra spend needed.

Email / Retention · illustrative example

Found: no abandoned checkout email flow running at all.

Fix: a simple 3-email abandoned checkout sequence.

Typical impact: this alone often recovers 5–10% of lost checkouts — close to free revenue.
