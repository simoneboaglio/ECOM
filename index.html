FAQ

Questions you're probably asking

Do you implement the changes?

No. I hand you the exact, prioritized roadmap and walk you through it personally, but you or your team make the changes. That's intentional — doing it for you would teach you nothing and just create dependency on me.

How is this different from an agency?

An agency usually sells you ongoing management of one channel — ads, or email, or CRO — and has an incentive to keep that retainer going. This is a one-time diagnosis across your whole funnel, with no retainer and no ongoing management. You get the analysis and the plan; you stay in control of execution.

What if I'm too early-stage or not making sales yet?

Honestly, this works best once you have real traffic and real order data — that's what makes the diagnosis data-driven instead of guesswork. If your store hasn't launched yet or you have no orders to analyze, this probably isn't the right fit yet. Come back once you have some real numbers to look at.

How long until I get my roadmap?

Typically within [X] business days of me receiving full access to your store and ad-account data. Exact timing depends on how complete the data is when I get it.

What happens after I apply?

I personally review every application to check it's a real fit. If it is, I'll follow up asking for deeper store and ad-account data (or screenshots) before anything gets scheduled — the diagnosis is only as good as the data behind it.
